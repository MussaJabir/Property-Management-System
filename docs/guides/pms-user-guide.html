Part 1

Landlords and staff

Everything here happens in the staff area at pms.bjptechnologies.co.tz/manage, on a computer or a phone.

1Signing in and your dashboard

Your first sign-in

  1. Open the email titled "Activate your … staff account" and press Activate my account. If a phone number was given, the same link also arrives by SMS.
  2. Choose your password. The link works for 72 hours. If it has expired, ask the workspace owner to resend it (section 12), or ask BJP if you are the owner.
  3. From then on, sign in at pms.bjptechnologies.co.tz/manage with your email and password. Capital letters in the email don't matter.
Forgot your password?There is no reset link on the sign-in page. Ask the workspace owner to press Resend invite next to your name under Team; you'll get a new activation link and choose a new password.

The dashboard

The dashboard is the first page after signing in. The four figures at the top tell you where you stand this month:

OccupancyThe share of your units that have a renter.
Collected this monthPayments recorded this month, against what was billed.
OutstandingEverything still owed on open invoices.
OverdueThe part of that which is past its due date.

Below them are your collections over six months, units by status, the five largest unpaid invoices and the latest payments. A short tour runs the first time you sign in; you can skip it.

3Locations, properties and units

Set this up once. A location is an area (region, district, ward), a property is a building or compound, and a unit is what you rent out: a room, apartment, business frame, office, shop or warehouse.

Add a property

  1. Go to Inventory → Properties and press New Property.
  2. Enter the Property name and choose its Location. If the location doesn't exist yet, press + beside the field to add it there.
  3. Choose the Type and Status, and add the street address and a description if you wish.
  4. Add photos (up to 5 MB each); they appear on your website. Press Create.

Add units

  1. Go to Inventory → Units and press New Unit.
  2. Choose the Property and give the unit a short Unit code, such as "Room 5" or "Frame 2A".
  3. Choose the Type. Leave Status as Vacant unless someone already lives there.
  4. Under Rent, enter the amount in whole shillings (350000 for TZS 350,000), the currency and the billing cycle.
  5. Optionally fill in Specs (bedrooms, bathrooms, size), tick Amenities and add photos. Press Create, or Create & create another to add the next unit straight away.
Plan limitsEach plan allows a number of properties, units and staff, and the Properties, Units and Team pages show how many you've used, for example "45 of 60 units used on the Starter plan". When you reach the limit, the New button is replaced by Request an upgrade, which tells BJP you need a bigger plan. Everything you already have keeps working.

4Adding renters

  1. Go to Leasing → Renters and press New Renter.
  2. Under Who is renting? choose an individual or a business and enter the Full name.
  3. Enter the Phone number. You can type it as 0712 345 678; PMS stores it in the international format. Add an email address if they have one.
  4. Add identification (NIDA or TIN number), an emergency contact and notes if you need them. Press Create.
Phone and email matter.The phone number is how the renter signs in to the renter portal. The email address is where their invoices, receipts and reminders are sent. Without an email, the renter still sees everything in the portal.

5Leases

A lease connects a renter to a unit, with the rent, dates and deposit. Creating one takes four steps.

  1. Go to Leasing → Leases and press New Lease.
  2. Renter: choose who is renting.
  3. Unit: choose which unit they're renting.
  4. Terms: enter the start and end dates, rent, deposit, billing cycle and the day of the month rent is due.
  5. Confirm: check the summary and create the lease. It starts as Pending.

Activating a lease

When the renter moves in, press Activate on the lease. Three things happen:

  • The lease becomes Active and the unit is marked Occupied.
  • If your plan includes the renter portal, the renter gets an account and an activation link by email (and SMS where set up).
  • The change is recorded in the lease's History.

Other lease actions

Download PDFA printable lease contract to sign.
End (natural)The lease ran its course. The unit becomes vacant again.
TerminateEnding early. You're asked for a reason, which is kept in the history.
HistoryEvery change to the lease, who made it and when.

6Invoices

Create an invoice for each rent period.PMS doesn't create rent invoices on its own yet. At the start of each period, create the invoice for each lease as described below. Overdue tracking is automatic.
  1. Go to Billing → Invoices and press New Invoice.
  2. Under Invoice header, choose the lease, the Period start and Period end, and the Due date. Leave the invoice number empty; it's given when you issue.
  3. Under Line items, press Add line for each charge. A line can be rent, a utility, a fee, a deposit or anything else.
  4. Press Create. The invoice is saved as a Draft, which the renter can't see yet, so you can still correct it.
  5. When it's right, press Issue on the invoice. It gets its number, and the renter is emailed and sees it in the portal.

What the statuses mean

DraftNot sent yet; only you can see it.
UnpaidIssued, nothing paid.
PartialPart of it is paid.
PaidFully paid.
OverduePast its due date and not fully paid. PMS checks every morning at 06:00 and emails the renter a reminder.
CancelledCancelled with Cancel; kept for your records.

Every invoice can be downloaded with Download PDF.

7Payments and receipts

Renters pay you the way they always have: cash, bank, mobile money. You record the payment in PMS, and the receipt is produced for you.

  1. Go to Billing → Payments and press New Payment.
  2. Under Which invoice? choose the invoice being paid.
  3. Enter the Amount and Payment date. A part payment is fine; the invoice becomes Partial.
  4. Choose the Method: cash, bank transfer, mobile money, cheque or card. Add the transaction or cheque number under Reference.
  5. Leave the status as Completed and press Create.

PMS then updates the invoice balance, creates a numbered receipt, and emails it to the renter as a PDF. You'll find every receipt under Billing → Receipts, where you can download or email it again.

8Expenses

Record what your buildings cost, such as repairs, security, cleaning and utilities, so your reports show real profit. Go to Operations → Expenses, press New Expense, and choose a category, the property, the amount and the date. You can attach a photo of the paper receipt. Manage your list of categories under Operations → Categories.

9Maintenance requests

Renters report problems from the renter portal, with photos if they like. Staff can also log a request with New Maintenance Request.

  • A new request notifies the owner, managers and maintenance staff by email and in the bell.
  • Press Start when work begins and Complete when it's done, or Cancel it. The renter sees each change in the portal.
  • Open a request to add progress notes, which keep a running record of what was done.

10Your website

Your workspace comes with a public website at pms.bjptechnologies.co.tz/your-workspace, with Home, About, Units, News and Contact pages.

  • Website → Pages: edit the text of each page.
  • Website → Announcements: post news, such as a water cut or new units.
  • Website → Contact inbox: messages visitors send through the contact form. You're also notified by email.
  • The Units page lists your vacant units with their photos, rent and amenities automatically. Once a unit is occupied it drops off.

12Your team and roles

  1. Go to Administration → Team and press Invite member.
  2. Enter their name and email and choose a role. They receive an activation email, as you did.
  3. If the email doesn't arrive or the link expires, press Resend invite next to their name. The new link is also shown to you, so you can send it by WhatsApp.
RoleTypical use
OwnerEverything, including the team and settings.
ManagerDay-to-day running: renters, leases, billing, maintenance.
AccountantInvoices, payments, expenses and reports.
Maintenance staffMaintenance requests.

On the Pro plan and above, the owner can change exactly what each role may do under Administration → Roles & Permissions.

13Workspace settings and your plan

Settings (owners only) has five tabs: Business (name, email, phone), Branding (logo and colour, used on your website and renter portal), Preferences, Security (change your password) and Subscription (your plan and when it renews).

Part 2 · For renters

Your renter portal

Your landlord uses PMS to manage your tenancy. The renter portal lets you see your rent, download receipts and report problems from your phone. Nothing to install: it opens in your browser.

14Activating your account

  1. When your lease starts, you receive an email (and an SMS where available) with an activation link.
  2. Open it and choose your password. The link works for 72 hours. If it has expired, ask your landlord to send a new one.

15Signing in

Go to the portal address your landlord gives you, for example pms.bjptechnologies.co.tz/mshale-plaza/portal. Sign in with your phone number (such as 0712345678) and your password.

Forgot your password?Contact your landlord. They'll send you a new activation link so you can choose a new one.

16Your dashboard and invoices

The Dashboard shows your unit, your next due date, what you owe in total, and your lease details. Under Invoices you'll see every invoice; filter by Open or Paid, and download the receipt for any payment.

Pay your rent the way your landlord asks: cash, bank or mobile money. Once they record it, your receipt arrives by email and appears here.

17Reporting a problem

  1. Open Maintenance and start a new request.
  2. Give it a short title, choose your unit and how urgent it is, and describe the problem. Add photos if they help.
  3. Press Submit request. Your landlord is notified straight away, and you can follow progress under Maintenance.

18Your profile and password

Under Profile you can update your details and change your password.
